PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Rudra Pratap's portfolio website. Your privacy and trust are paramount. As a cybersecurity specialist and designer, I implement strict privacy-by-design standards. This policy outlines how information is collected, protected, and used.

2. Information Collected

This portfolio is primarily informative. The only personal data collected occurs when you voluntarily:

  • Submit a Review / Feedback: Your name, designation/role, rating, and feedback text submitted through the public review modal.
  • Contact via Email or WhatsApp: Any information you choose to provide in direct email communications or messages.

3. How Your Information is Used

  • Public Testimonials: Approved reviews are displayed on the testimonials section with your name and role to demonstrate client collaborations.
  • Professional Communication: Replying to internship, collaboration, and freelance project inquiries.
  • Spam Prevention & Security: We employ automated bot/honeypot filtering and rate limiting to prevent DDoS attacks and spam submissions.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use minimal client-side local storage exclusively for essential user experience preferences:

  • Theme Preference: Storing your preferred Light or Dark mode toggle.
  • Cookie Consent: Remembering whether you acknowledged the cookie notification banner.

No intrusive third-party cross-site advertising trackers or tracking cookies are utilized.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption, HTTPS TLS protocols, secure MongoDB cloud databases, and strictly parameterized backend APIs to protect all submitted data against unauthorized access.
